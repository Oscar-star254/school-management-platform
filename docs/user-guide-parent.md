# Parent and learner guide

1. Open the app and choose **Student**.
2. Enter the learner admission number and school-issued password.
3. Use **Home** for the current level, attendance, balance, and notices.
4. Open **Results** for learning-area marks and CBC performance levels.
5. Open **Reports** and choose **Download PDF** to keep a progress report offline.
6. Open **Fees** for invoice lines and payment instructions.
7. Open **More** for assignments, materials, attendance, password, and storage.

The first login needs internet. A remembered session can reopen offline. Payments always require a connection.
